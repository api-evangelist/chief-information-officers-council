# Chief Information Officers Council

> The Chief Information Officers Council (CIOC) is the principal interagency forum for improving
> agency practices related to the design, acquisition, development, modernization, use, sharing and
> performance of federal information resources. Established by Executive Order 13011 (1996) and
> codified in the E-Government Act of 2002 (44 U.S.C. 3603), it is made up of the CIOs and Deputy
> CIOs of executive branch agencies, chaired by the Federal CIO at OMB. The Council publishes
> guidance, playbooks, committee output and its charter as web pages and PDFs. It does NOT operate a
> developer program, publish a machine-readable API contract, or expose a callable API host.

## What the council publishes

- [About CIOC](https://www.councils.gov/cioc/): Charter, mission and committee structure on
  Councils.gov, the consolidated federal executive councils hub.
- [CIOC Resources Library](https://www.councils.gov/resources/?council=CIOC): Playbooks, guidance and
  policy resources filtered to the CIO Council.
- [CIOC News & Events](https://www.councils.gov/news-events/?council=CIOC): Council announcements and
  event listings.
- [CIOC Leadership & Members](https://www.councils.gov/cioc/members-leaders/): Current chair, vice
  chair and member roster.
- [CIO Council Charter, December 2020 (PDF)](https://www.councils.gov/docs/CIOC-Charter-Dec-2020.pdf):
  The council's governing charter.

## Machine-readable surface

There is none to point at, and that is a measured result rather than an unchecked assumption.
Probed 2026-09-05 across www.councils.gov, councils.gov, cio.gov and tmf.cio.gov: no OpenAPI or
Swagger at /openapi.json, /openapi.yaml, /swagger.json, /api-docs or /redoc; no GraphQL endpoint;
no MCP server; no A2A agent card at /.well-known/agent-card.json or /.well-known/agent.json; no
apis.json at /apis.json, /apis.yml or /.well-known/apis.json; no llms.txt. See
`well-known/chief-information-officers-council-well-known.yml` in the profile repository for the
full probe record including negative controls.

Note on legacy links: cio.gov was retired in March 2026 when GSA consolidated the interagency
council websites into councils.gov. cio.gov now 301-redirects to https://www.councils.gov/cioc/,
and https://www.cio.gov/ fails TLS validation. Prefer councils.gov URLs.
